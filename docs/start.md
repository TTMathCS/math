# Start here: first lessons

*Six first lessons for young beginners, one from each of six topics. Pick any one, or go in order. Each lesson ends with where it leads; when you like a topic, climb its ladder of 8 levels.*

| | Lesson | The question | Its topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Counting things](topics/numbers/level-1/01-counting-things.md) | why two people can count differently | [Numbers](topics/numbers/index.md) |
| 2 | [Circles, triangles and squares](topics/geometry/level-1/01-naming-shapes.md) | name every shape you see | [Geometry](topics/geometry/index.md) |
| 3 | [Repeating patterns](topics/patterns/level-1/01-repeating-patterns.md) | what comes next? | [Patterns](topics/patterns/index.md) |
| 4 | [Dressing a teddy](topics/counting/level-1/01-dressing-teddy.md) | how many different outfits? | [Counting](topics/counting/index.md) |
| 5 | [Sure, maybe, never](topics/probability/level-1/01-sure-maybe-never.md) | how likely is it? | [Probability](topics/probability/index.md) |
| 6 | [The biggest number game](topics/infinity/level-1/01-biggest-number-game.md) | is there a biggest number? | [Infinity](topics/infinity/index.md) |

There are 13 topics in all: see [every topic](topics/index.md).

For parents and teachers: level 1 suits most children from about 5, level 3 from about 9, level 5 from about 12, level 7 from about 15. Every lesson works on paper, and the answers to the questions are folded until you tap them.
