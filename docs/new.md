# What's new

*Recent results in mathematics, newest first, a few lines each with a link. Some are easy to state and good for a classroom. Last checked: 4 November 2026.*

| When | What changed | More |
|---|---|---|
| July 2025 | **AI at the Mathematical Olympiad.** AI systems reach gold-medal level at the IMO, solving five of six problems with written proofs; a year earlier one had reached silver with proofs checked in the Lean proof assistant. | [Where logic goes](books/logic/20-Where-Logic-Goes.md) |
| February 2025 | **The Kakeya conjecture in three dimensions** is proved by Hong Wang and Joshua Zahl: a set in which a needle can point in every direction must still be "fully three-dimensional". | [Where analysis goes](books/calculus/22-Where-Analysis-Goes.md) |
| November 2024 | **The moving sofa problem**: Jineon Baek posts a proof that Gerver's 1992 sofa is the largest that turns a right-angled corner in a hallway (claimed; experts are checking it). | [Where geometry goes](books/geometry/21-Where-Geometry-Goes.md) |
| October 2024 | **A new largest known prime**, $2^{136279841} - 1$, with 41,024,320 digits, found by Luke Durant using cloud GPUs. | [Where number theory goes](books/number-theory/21-Where-Number-Theory-Goes.md) |
| July 2024 | **The fifth Busy Beaver number**, BB(5) = 47,176,870, is settled by an online collaboration, with a computer-checked proof in Coq. | [Where logic goes](books/logic/20-Where-Logic-Goes.md) |
| May 2024 | **The geometric Langlands conjecture** is proved by Gaitsgory, Raskin and seven others, in about 800 pages. | [Where algebra goes](books/algebra/22-Where-Algebra-Goes.md#225-the-langlands-programme) |
| March 2023 | **The "hat"**: Smith, Myers, Kaplan and Goodman-Strauss find a single tile that covers the plane only in patterns that never repeat (an "einstein"), then the "spectre", which needs no mirror images. | [Geometry topic](topics/geometry/index.md) |
| March 2023 | **Ramsey numbers**: Campos, Griffiths, Morris and Sahasrabudhe give the first exponential improvement in 80 years on the upper bound for diagonal Ramsey numbers. | [Ramsey theory](books/combinatorics/16-Ramsey-Theory.md) |
