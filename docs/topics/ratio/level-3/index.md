# Level 3: Ratios and rates

*Ratio, rates and money · level 3 of 8 · rough age 9-10 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 2 you said "for every" and filled in ratio tables. In this level that idea gets its own notation, $3 : 2$,
and its own rules: why multiplying both parts keeps a mix the same while adding does not, how to share any amount
fairly in a ratio with a bar model, and how to compare things of different kinds with a rate. You will find the best
buy in a shop, work out speeds and travel times (even to Mars), and finish by planning a real party budget.

| [Sharing in a ratio](03-sharing-in-ratio.md) | Share an amount in a ratio with a bar model, even when you know only one share or the difference |

| [Planning a budget for an event](06-event-budget.md) | Plan a party within a fixed amount, estimate safely, and fix a plan that goes over |
