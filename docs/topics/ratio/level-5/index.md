# Level 5: Proportional reasoning

*Ratio, rates and money · level 5 of 8 · rough age 12-13 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 4 you learned what a percent is and how to find one. In this level you reason with ratios like a
mathematician: you solve proportions three ways and learn why cross multiplying works, tell direct proportion from
inverse proportion (and from neither), and measure change as a percent. Then you put it to work on real money
(tips, markups, commission, exchange rates and simple interest) and on gears, paint and recipes.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Proportions](01-proportions.md) | Solve a proportion by scaling, by unit rate and by cross multiplying, and explain why cross multiplying works |
| [Direct and inverse proportion](02-direct-inverse.md) | Tell direct, inverse and neither apart from words, tables and graphs, and solve problems with each |
| [Percent increase and decrease](03-percent-change.md) | Find a percent change from the original, explain why up 25% and down 20% can undo each other, and find a new amount |
| [Commission, tips and markups](04-commission-tips-markups.md) | Work out tips in your head, markups and margins, and salary, straight and tiered commission |
| [Exchange rates](05-exchange-rates.md) | Convert between currencies both ways, use the inverse rate, and explain what the buying and selling rates cost you |
| [Simple interest](06-simple-interest.md) | Use $I = Prt$ for savings and short loans, for months and days, and solve for any of its letters |
| [Gears, mixtures and recipes](07-gears-mixtures.md) | Find how fast meshing gears turn, mix paint and juice to a ratio or a percent, and scale a recipe |
