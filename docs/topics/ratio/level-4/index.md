# Level 4: Percent

*Ratio, rates and money · level 4 of 8 · rough age 11-12 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

A percent is a ratio with the second number fixed at 100, and that one small idea makes comparing almost anything
easy. In this level you see percents on a hundred grid and move between fractions, decimals and percents; find a
percent of any amount in your head; turn test scores and survey results into percents; and then use it all with real
money: sales tax at the till, discounts in a sale, and a monthly budget that balances.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Percent means per hundred](01-per-hundred.md) | Show a percent on a hundred grid and write the same amount as a fraction, a decimal and a percent |
| [Percent of an amount](02-percent-of-amount.md) | Find any percent of an amount by building from 10%, 5% and 1%, or with a decimal, and explain why $a\%$ of $b$ equals $b\%$ of $a$ |
| [One amount as a percent of another](03-as-a-percent.md) | Write "this out of that" as a percent and compare results with different totals |
| [Sales tax](04-sales-tax.md) | Add 13% HST (or 5% GST) to a price or a bill, round for cash, and find a price before tax |
| [Discounts and sale prices](05-discounts-sale-prices.md) | Find a sale price in one step, compare a percent off with an amount off, and see through "buy one, get one" deals |
| [Budgets](06-monthly-budget.md) | Build a monthly budget with fixed and variable costs, show its parts as percents, and fix one that does not balance |
