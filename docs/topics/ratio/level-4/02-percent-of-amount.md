# Percent of an amount

*Ratio, rates and money · level 4, lesson 2 of 6*

## A question to start

Which is bigger: **8% of 25**, or **25% of 8**?

Most people reach for a calculator. But you can do both in your head in a few seconds, and the answer is a small
surprise: they are **exactly the same**. Both are 2.

Is that a coincidence? Would 7% of 40 and 40% of 7 also match? By the end of this lesson you will know a few fast
ways to find any percent of any amount, and you will be able to explain why the swap always works.

## The idea

### "Of" means multiply

You already know that "half of 10" is 5, which is $\tfrac12 \times 10$. In math, the little word **of** between a
fraction and an amount means **multiply**. Percents are fractions with 100 at the bottom, so

$$35\% \text{ of } 80 = \frac{35}{100} \times 80.$$

That is the whole idea. The rest of this lesson is about doing it **quickly** and **sensibly**.

### Start from 100%

100% of an amount is the whole amount. From there, a few easy steps get you anywhere.

- **50%**: halve it. 50% of 80 is 40.
- **25%**: halve again. 25% of 80 is 20.
- **10%**: divide by 10. 10% of 80 is 8.
- **5%**: halve the 10%. 5% of 80 is 4.
- **1%**: divide by 100. 1% of 80 is 0.8.

Why is 10% "divide by 10"? Because 10% is $\tfrac{10}{100} = \tfrac{1}{10}$. On the hundred grid, 10% is one column out
of ten equal columns. Sharing 80 equally into 10 columns puts 8 in each.

### Build up from pieces

Any percent can be built from these pieces, the way you build an amount of money from coins.

$$35\% = 10\% + 10\% + 10\% + 5\%$$

So 35% of 80 is $8 + 8 + 8 + 4 = 28$.

go together.

Some other useful builds:

- $15\% = 10\% + 5\%$.
- $20\% = 10\% + 10\%$, or $10\%$ doubled.
- $90\% = 100\% - 10\%$ (sometimes taking away is faster than adding).
- $12.5\%$ is half of $25\%$.
- $2\% = 1\% + 1\%$.

### Using decimals

The build-up method is great in your head. With a calculator, or with awkward percents like 37%, change the percent
to a decimal and multiply:

$$35\% \text{ of } 80 = 0.35 \times 80 = 28.$$

This is the same calculation as before, because $0.35 = \tfrac{35}{100}$. Two different routes, one answer.

### Why the swap works

Back to the opening puzzle. Write both with "of means multiply":

$$8\% \text{ of } 25 = \frac{8}{100} \times 25 = \frac{8 \times 25}{100}, \qquad 25\% \text{ of } 8 = \frac{25}{100} \times 8 = \frac{25 \times 8}{100}.$$

Both are "the two numbers multiplied together, then divided by 100". And $8 \times 25 = 25 \times 8$, because
multiplying works in either order. So the answers **must** be equal: both are $\tfrac{200}{100} = 2$.

So you can always swap: **$a\%$ of $b$ equals $b\%$ of $a$**. Use whichever is easier. 4% of 75 sounds hard; 75% of 4
is obviously 3. And 7% of 40 is 40% of 7, which is $0.4 \times 7 = 2.8$.

### Percents over 100

The same tools work for percents bigger than 100. 150% of 40 is $100\% + 50\%$, so $40 + 20 = 60$. With a decimal:
$1.5 \times 40 = 60$. Taking more than 100% of something gives more than you started with.

### Does the answer make sense?

Always do a quick sense check:

- A percent **under 100%** gives an answer **smaller** than the amount.
- 50% is half, 25% is a quarter, 10% is a tenth: compare your answer with these.
- 35% is a bit more than a third, so 35% of 80 should be a bit more than $80 \div 3 \approx 27$. And 28 is. ✓

## Worked examples

**1. A mental build.** Find 15% of 60.

- 10% of 60 is 6. 5% is half of that, 3.
- 15% is $6 + 3 = 9$.

**2. Taking away.** Find 90% of 250.

- 10% of 250 is 25.
- 90% is $250 - 25 = 225$.

**3. Using a decimal.** Find 37% of 450.

- $0.37 \times 450 = 166.5$.
- Sense check: 37% is a bit under 40%, and 40% of 450 is $4 \times 45 = 180$. Our answer is a bit under 180. ✓

**4. The swap.** Find 4% of 75 and 16% of 25.

- 4% of 75 is 75% of 4, which is 3.
- 16% of 25 is 25% of 16, a quarter of 16, which is 4.

**5. A small percent.** Find 2.5% of 360.

- 10% is 36, so 5% is 18, and 2.5% is 9.

**6. A survey.** In a class of 30, 40% walk to school, 30% take the bus, and the rest come by car. How many come by
car?

- The percents must add to 100%, so by car is $100\% - 40\% - 30\% = 30\%$.
- 10% of 30 is 3, so 30% is 9 children.
- Check the whole class: walking is 40% of 30, which is 12; bus is 9; car is 9. $12 + 9 + 9 = 30$. ✓

## Try it

1. Find 10% of 70, of 340 and of 5.
2. Find 50%, 25% and 1% of 240.
3. Find 15% of 80 without a calculator.
4. Find 45% of 60 without a calculator.
5. Find 65% of 120 using a decimal.
6. Find 4% of 75 and 8% of 50. (Try the swap.)
7. Find 120% of 35.
8. A shop gives 20% of the price of every book to a library fund. How much goes to the fund from a book costing 14.50
   dollars? From three books at 14.50 dollars each?

??? note "Answers"
    1. $70 \div 10 = 7$; $340 \div 10 = 34$; $5 \div 10 = 0.5$.
    2. 50% is 120; 25% is 60; 1% is 2.4.
    3. 10% is 8 and 5% is 4, so 15% is $8 + 4 = 12$.
    4. 10% is 6, so 40% is 24; 5% is 3. 45% is $24 + 3 = 27$. (Or: 50% is 30, minus 5% which is 3, gives 27.)
    5. $0.65 \times 120 = 78$. Sense check: a bit under two thirds of 120 (which is 80). ✓
    6. 4% of 75 is 75% of 4, which is **3**. 8% of 50 is 50% of 8, which is **4**.
    7. 100% is 35 and 20% is 7, so 120% is $35 + 7 = 42$. (Decimal: $1.2 \times 35 = 42$.)
    8. 10% of 14.50 is 1.45, so 20% is **2.90 dollars**. For three books: $3 \times 2.90 = 8.70$ dollars (or 20% of
       $3 \times 14.50 = 43.50$, which is also 8.70 dollars).

## Common mistake

**Dividing by the percent.** "15% of 60" is not $60 \div 15 = 4$. A percent is a number of hundredths, so you multiply
by $\tfrac{15}{100}$. The only percent that works by dividing by its own number is 10% (divide by 10), and that is
because $10\% = \tfrac{1}{10}$.

**Writing the decimal wrong.** 5% of 80 is $0.05 \times 80 = 4$, not $0.5 \times 80 = 40$. A sense check catches it:
5% is tiny, so the answer must be small.
