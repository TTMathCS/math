# Level 7: Growth, decay and annuities

*Ratio, rates and money · level 7 of 8 · rough age 15-16 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 6 you multiplied by the same percent again and again. In this level that one idea describes populations,
medicines, cooling drinks and radioactive atoms, and you learn to measure it by doubling times and half-lives. Then
you take money seriously across time: compounding monthly or daily, the present value of a future amount, and
annuities, the streams of regular payments behind every savings plan and loan, added up as geometric series. The level
ends with the numbers of a whole financial life: tax brackets, pay cheques, budgets and net worth.
