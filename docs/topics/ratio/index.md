# Ratio and rates

A ratio compares two amounts: two cups of flour for every cup of sugar, one centimetre on the map for every
kilometre on the ground. A rate compares amounts of different things, such as kilometres and hours. Together they
let you scale a recipe, read a map, compare prices and describe anything that grows or shrinks.
