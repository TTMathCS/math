# Level 8: Loans, investing and e

*Ratio, rates and money · level 8 of 8 · rough age 17 and beyond (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 7 you valued streams of payments with geometric series. In this level you take loans apart payment by
payment, work through a Canadian mortgage, and look at investing with honest averages, bond prices and the long reach
of fees. You separate what money says from what it buys, follow compounding to its limit and meet the number $e$, and
end where ratio began, with shape: the paper sizes that halve perfectly and the true and false stories of the golden
ratio. All of it is mathematics, not financial advice.
