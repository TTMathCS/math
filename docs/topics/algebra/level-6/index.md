# Level 6: Systems and polynomials

*Algebra · level 6 of 8 · rough age 13-14 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 5 every equation had one unknown and every expression was a straight line in disguise. This level opens two
new doors. The first is **polynomials**: expressions with $x^2$, $x^3$ and beyond, which you learn to multiply with an
area picture and then to factor, running the picture backwards. The second is **systems**: two equations with two
unknowns, solved by drawing two lines and finding where they cross, then by substitution and elimination. The level
ends by learning to tell straight-line relations from curved ones, which leads into the parabolas of level 7.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Exponent laws with variables](01-exponent-laws.md) | Explain and use the laws for $x^a x^b$, $(x^a)^b$ and $(xy)^n$, and simplify $(2x^3y)^2 \div 4x^2y$ |
| [Polynomials](02-polynomials.md) | Name the parts of a polynomial, add and subtract them, and multiply two brackets with an area model |
| [Factoring](03-factoring.md) | Factor common factors, $x^2 + bx + c$, $ax^2 + bx + c$, differences of squares and perfect squares, and check by expanding |
| [Linear systems by graphing](04-systems-graphing.md) | Solve two equations by drawing two lines, and explain why there is one solution, none or infinitely many |
| [Linear systems by substitution and elimination](05-substitution-elimination.md) | Solve systems exactly with either method, choose the better one, and solve mixture and speed problems |
| [Linear and non-linear relations](06-linear-nonlinear.md) | Tell linear from non-linear by table, graph and equation, and choose a line or a curve to model real data |
