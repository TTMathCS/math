# Level 8: Advanced functions

*Algebra · level 8 of 8 · rough age 17 and beyond (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

The last level of algebra is the bridge to university mathematics. It studies whole families of functions:
polynomials of any degree and how to factor them, rational functions with their asymptotes, and logarithms, the
inverse of exponentials, which bring the unknown down from the exponent. Then it combines functions, proves the
compound angle formulas of trigonometry and solves trigonometric equations, and finally leaves the flat page to
describe lines and planes in three dimensions.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Polynomial functions](01-polynomial-functions.md) | Predict end behaviour and the shape at each zero, divide polynomials (long and synthetic), and use the remainder and factor theorems to factor cubics |
| [Polynomial inequalities and families](02-polynomial-inequalities.md) | Solve inequalities like $(x - 1)(x + 2)(x - 4) > 0$ with a sign chart, and pick one member of a family of polynomials with given zeros |
| [Rational functions](03-rational-functions.md) | Find vertical and horizontal asymptotes and holes, and solve rational equations and inequalities without losing or inventing solutions |
| [Logarithms](04-logarithms.md) | Read $\log_b x$ as an exponent, prove the laws of logarithms from the exponent laws, change base, and graph $\log x$ as the inverse of $10^x$ |
| [Exponential and logarithmic equations](05-exp-log-equations.md) | Solve $3^x = 20$ and $\log x + \log(x - 3) = 1$, reject extraneous roots, and model half-life, pH and decibels |
| [Combining functions](06-combining-functions.md) | Add, subtract, multiply, divide and compose functions, find the domain of $f(g(x))$, and break a function into simpler pieces |
| [Trigonometric identities and equations](07-trig-identities-equations.md) | Prove the compound and double angle formulas, prove identities, and solve equations like $2\sin^2 x - \sin x - 1 = 0$ on $[0, 2\pi]$ |
| [Lines and planes in space](08-lines-planes-space.md) | Write vector and parametric equations of lines, use the cross product, find the equation of a plane, and solve three equations as three planes |
