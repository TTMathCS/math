# Level 5: Equations and linear relations

*Algebra · level 5 of 8 · rough age 12-13 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 4 you solved equations with the letter on one side. In this level the equations grow up: brackets, the
letter on both sides, fractions, and whole formulas to rearrange. Then algebra meets the real world. You describe a
situation that changes at a steady rate with a table, a graph and an equation, solve inequalities with the full set of
rules (including the famous sign flip), and turn stories into equations whose answers you check for sense.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [The distributive law](01-distributive-law.md) | Expand $3(x + 4)$ and $-2(x - 5)$, explain why with an area picture, and take out a common factor |
| [Equations with brackets and x on both sides](02-brackets-both-sides.md) | Solve $5x - 3 = 2x + 9$, equations with brackets and equations with fractions, and spot equations with no solution or every solution |
| [Rearranging formulas](03-rearranging-formulas.md) | Make any letter the subject: $r$ in $C = 2\pi r$, $C$ in $F = \tfrac{9}{5}C + 32$, $t$ in $v = u + at$ |
| [Linear relations](04-linear-relations.md) | Describe one situation with a table, a graph and an equation, and tell direct variation from partial variation |
| [Solving inequalities](05-solving-inequalities.md) | Solve two-step and compound inequalities like $-3 < 2x + 1 \le 7$, explain the sign flip, and show the answer on a line |
| [Modelling with equations](06-modelling-equations.md) | Turn a story into an equation, solve it, and check that the answer makes sense |
