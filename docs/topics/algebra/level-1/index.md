# Level 1: Mystery numbers

*Algebra · level 1 of 8 · rough age 5-6 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Algebra starts with a game every child already knows: "I'm thinking of a number." In this level a hidden number is
found from clues, from a balance scale and from counters under a cup. You learn what the equal sign really means
(not "the answer comes next", but "both sides are the same"), and you play detective with secret sorting rules. No
letters yet: just numbers, blocks, cups and careful thinking.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [I'm thinking of a number](01-thinking-of-number.md) | Find a mystery number from clues like "one more than 4", and check a guess against every clue |
| [Balance scales](02-balance-scales.md) | Say which way a scale tips, and keep it level by doing the same to both sides |
| [The equal sign means "the same as"](03-equal-sign.md) | Read = as "the same amount as", and make both sides of a number sentence match with blocks |
| [Missing numbers](04-missing-numbers.md) | Find how many counters are hidden under a cup, as in 3 + ? = 5, and check the answer |
| [What is the rule?](05-what-is-rule.md) | Guess a secret sorting rule from examples, test it, and know why one "no" can rule a guess out |
