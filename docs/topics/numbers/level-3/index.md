# Level 3: Big numbers, times tables and fractions

*Numbers · level 3 of 8 · rough age 9-10 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 2 you worked with numbers up to 1000 and met multiplying and sharing. In this level the numbers grow to
millions and billions, the times tables stop being a list to memorise and become a web of facts that hold each
other up, and you learn to multiply and divide numbers of any size, knowing **why** each step works. Then the
numbers go the other way, into the gaps between whole numbers: fractions and decimals.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Numbers to a million and beyond](01-millions-and-beyond.md) | Read, write, compare and round numbers in the millions and billions, and feel how big a million really is |
| [Times tables with understanding](02-times-tables.md) | Build any times-table fact from easier ones, spot the patterns in the tables, and multiply by 10, 100 and 1000 |
| [Multiplying bigger numbers](03-multi-digit-multiplication.md) | Multiply two- and three-digit numbers with the area model and the standard method, and estimate first |
| [Division with remainders](04-long-division.md) | Divide by sharing hundreds, tens and ones, do long division, and decide what a remainder means in a story |
| [Fractions are numbers](05-fractions-as-numbers.md) | Place fractions on the number line, and switch between improper fractions and mixed numbers |
| [Equal and comparing fractions](06-equivalent-fractions.md) | Make equivalent fractions, explain why they are equal, and compare any two fractions |
| [Decimals: tenths and hundredths](07-decimals.md) | Read, write and compare decimals, and see them as fractions out of 10 and 100 |
