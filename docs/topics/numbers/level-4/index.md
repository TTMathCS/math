# Level 4: Working with fractions and decimals

*Numbers · level 4 of 8 · rough age 11-12 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 3 you met fractions and decimals as numbers: you placed them on the number line and compared them. In this
level you calculate with them: add, subtract, multiply and divide, and for every rule you learn **why** it works, so
you never have to trust a rule you cannot explain. Then the number line grows to the left with the negative numbers,
and the level ends with the agreement that tells everyone in the world how to read a long calculation the same way.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Adding and subtracting fractions](01-adding-fractions.md) | Explain why the denominators must match, find a common denominator, and add and subtract mixed numbers |
| [Multiplying fractions](02-multiplying-fractions.md) | Find a fraction of a fraction with the area model, and explain why multiplying can make a number smaller |
| [Dividing fractions](03-dividing-fractions.md) | Divide by a fraction, and explain two ways why "multiply by the reciprocal" works |
| [Calculating with decimals](04-decimal-operations.md) | Add, subtract, multiply and divide decimals, and explain where the decimal point goes |
| [Fractions to decimals and back](05-fractions-to-decimals.md) | Turn any fraction into a decimal, and predict whether the decimal stops or repeats |
| [Negative numbers](06-negative-numbers.md) | Compare negative numbers, and add and subtract them with chips and on the number line |
| [Order of operations](07-order-of-operations.md) | Work out long calculations in the agreed order, and explain why the agreement is needed |
