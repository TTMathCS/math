# Level 6: The real numbers

*Numbers · level 6 of 8 · rough age 13-14 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Fractions seem to fill the number line, but they do not: some points, like $\sqrt2$, can never be written as a
fraction. In this level you find out exactly which decimals are fractions, prove (not just check) that $\sqrt2$ is
irrational, and learn to calculate exactly with roots. Then you put every family of numbers into one picture, the real
numbers, and finish with the practical side: how to round, how big errors can grow, and a 2000-year-old method that
finds square roots astonishingly fast.
