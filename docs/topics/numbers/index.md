# Numbers

Numbers are the oldest tool in mathematics. People first counted with marks on sticks; later they found ways to
write huge numbers, share things into parts, and even name numbers that can never be written out in full. This
ladder climbs from counting tricks to the complex numbers, where $-1$ finally gets a square root.

| Step | Page | You will be able to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Play | [Counting, huge numbers and old ways to write them](01-play.md) | Count in groups, feel how big a million is, and read Roman and Mayan numerals |
| Explore | [Place value, decimals and negative numbers](02-explore.md) | Read and round any whole number or decimal, and use negative numbers on the number line |
| Build | [Fractions, decimals and percentages](03-build.md) | Simplify, compare, add, multiply and divide fractions, and switch between fractions, decimals and percent |
| Master | [Powers, roots and the real numbers](04-master.md) | Use the rules of exponents and scientific notation, simplify roots, and prove that $\sqrt2$ is irrational |
| Beyond | [Complex numbers](05-beyond.md) | Calculate with $i = \sqrt{-1}$, draw complex numbers on a plane and solve every quadratic equation |
