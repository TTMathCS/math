# Level 2: Tens, hundreds and sharing

*Numbers · level 2 of 8 · rough age 7-8 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 1 you learned that ten ones make a ten. In this level the groups get bigger: ten tens make a hundred, and
place value lets you read, write, add and subtract numbers up to a thousand, with regrouping that you can explain, not
just follow. Then come two new operations, multiplying as equal groups and dividing as fair sharing, and the first
fractions: halves, thirds and quarters. The level ends with a tour of older ways to write numbers, and why ours won.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Place value to 1000](01-place-value.md) | Read, write and compare any number to 1000, say what each digit is worth, and explain why zero holds a place |
| [Adding and subtracting two- and three-digit numbers](02-adding-subtracting.md) | Add and subtract by splitting, by jumping, and in columns with regrouping, and check subtraction with addition |
| [Fact families and mental tricks](03-fact-families.md) | Turn one fact into four, use doubles, near doubles and "add 10, take 1", and estimate to check |
| [Multiplying as equal groups](04-equal-groups.md) | Find products by skip counting and arrays, and explain why 3 × 4 = 4 × 3 |
| [Dividing as fair sharing and as making groups](05-fair-sharing.md) | Divide by sharing and by grouping, find leftovers, and use multiplying to divide |
| [Halves, thirds and quarters](06-halves-thirds-quarters.md) | Cut shapes and groups into equal parts, name and compare unit fractions, and place them on a number line |
| [Old ways to write numbers](07-old-numerals.md) | Read and write tally marks, Egyptian, Roman and Maya numerals, and explain why place value and zero matter |
