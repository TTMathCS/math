# Level 2: Trees and lists

*Counting and combinations · level 2 of 8 · rough age 7-8 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 1 you counted with tidy lists and boxes. In this level the lists grow into **tree diagrams**, and the trees
turn into a quick multiplication. You learn when to multiply and when to add, count codes and locks, find the
handshake numbers hiding in the diagonals of a shape, and hunt down every triangle and rectangle in a picture. Each
time, you also learn **why** the count is right.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Tree diagrams](01-tree-diagrams.md) | Draw a tree for choices made in steps, read a path as one result, and explain why counting tips counts results |
| [From trees to multiplying](02-trees-to-multiplying.md) | Count the tips of an even tree by multiplying (3 × 2 × 2 = 12), and spot an uneven tree where you must add |
| ["And" multiplies, "or" adds](03-and-or.md) | Decide between adding and multiplying from the words of a problem, use both together, and avoid double counting |
| [Codes and locks](04-codes-and-locks.md) | Count codes with repeats (100 two-digit codes, 27 three-letter words) and without, and see why each dial multiplies by 10 |
| [Handshakes and diagonals](05-handshakes-diagonals.md) | Find the handshake numbers by multiplying and halving, and use them to count the lines between points and the diagonals of any shape |
| [Counting shapes in a picture](06-counting-shapes.md) | Count every triangle and rectangle in a picture by size, and check the count by choosing lines |
