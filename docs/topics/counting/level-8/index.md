# Level 8: Clever counting

*Counting and combinations · level 8 of 8 · rough age 17 and beyond (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

The earlier levels gave you formulas: $n!$, $\dbinom{n}{k}$, stars and bars. This last level is about the problems no
single formula answers, and the clever ideas mathematicians invented for them: counting by **recursion** (build the
answer for $n$ from smaller answers), **reflection** and **inclusion-exclusion**, pictures made of dots, polynomials
that **store** counts, and counting **up to symmetry**. These are the first steps of the subject called
combinatorics, studied at university and used everywhere from computer science to chemistry.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Counting with recursion](01-counting-recursion.md) | Set up and solve a recurrence for tilings, staircases and the Tower of Hanoi, and prove the Hanoi count by induction |
| [Catalan numbers](02-catalan-numbers.md) | Recognise Catalan problems (brackets, mountain paths, triangulations) and prove $C_n = \frac{1}{n+1}\binom{2n}{n}$ with the reflection trick |
| [Derangements](03-derangements.md) | Count the arrangements in which nothing is in its own place by inclusion-exclusion, and explain why the fraction tends to $1/e$ |
| [Partitions of a number](04-partitions.md) | Count the ways to write a number as a sum, draw Ferrers diagrams, and show why odd parts and distinct parts give the same count |
| [Generating functions](05-generating-functions.md) | Store a counting sequence in a polynomial, multiply polynomials to combine choices, and find the Sicherman dice |
| [Counting with symmetry, properly](06-burnside-lemma.md) | Count necklaces and coloured cubes up to rotation by averaging fixed colourings (Burnside's lemma) |
