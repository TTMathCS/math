# Level 1: Does it ever end?

*Infinity and strange math · level 1 of 8 · rough age 5-6 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Some things stop and some things go on forever. In this level you find out why there is no biggest number, why a
cookie you only ever eat half of is never finished, and why a circle has no end even though it is small. You look into
mirrors that make copies of copies, tell stories that start themselves again, and learn to tell **very big** apart
from **infinite**.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [The biggest number game](01-biggest-number-game.md) | Win the biggest number game by adding one, and explain why there is no biggest number and why infinity is not a number at the end |
| [Halving a cookie](02-halving-cookie.md) | Halve again and again, say how much is left, and explain why a crumb is always left and why all the pieces still fit in one cookie |
| [Going round and round](03-round-and-round.md) | Tell a line with ends from a loop with no end, count laps, go round the clock and the week, and see that "no end" is not "infinitely long" |
| [Mirrors facing each other](04-mirrors-facing.md) | Explain how two mirrors make copies of copies, why the copies shrink, and why real mirrors do not go on forever |
| [Stories that never end](05-never-ending-stories.md) | Draw a loop story with boxes and arrows, find the arrow that makes it a loop, and add a stopping rule |
| [Big but not infinite](06-big-not-infinite.md) | Use the "could I finish counting?" test to tell finite from infinite, and count big piles in tens and hundreds |
