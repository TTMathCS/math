# Level 2: Strange loops

*Infinity and strange math · level 2 of 8 · rough age 7-8 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In this level the never-ending things from level 1 start to play tricks. You make a loop of paper with only one side
and cut it in surprising ways, learn to compare groups by matching instead of counting, and use matching to find that
there are just as many even numbers as counting numbers. You meet the googol and the googolplex, enormous numbers that
are still not infinite, draw a picture that contains itself, and find out why the impossible triangle and the
never-ending staircase work on paper but never in real life.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [The Möbius strip](01-mobius-strip.md) | Make a Möbius strip, show it has one side and one edge, predict what cutting it does, and explain the surprises with lanes |
| [Matching without counting](02-matching-without-counting.md) | Compare two groups by matching them one to one, read the answer from the leftovers, and explain why counting is matching |
| [Patterns that go on forever](03-patterns-forever.md) | Match the counting numbers with the even numbers, the tens and the odd numbers, and explain why a part of an endless list can be as big as the whole |
| [Googol and googolplex](04-googol-googolplex.md) | Explain why a zero on the end makes a number ten times bigger, write a googol, and say why a googolplex is huge but still finite |
| [Picture in a picture](05-picture-in-picture.md) | Find the Droste effect around you, work out how fast the copies shrink, and draw your own picture in a picture |
| [Impossible pictures](06-impossible-pictures.md) | Explain why the Penrose triangle and the never-ending staircase cannot be built, by following the loop and counting the steps up |
