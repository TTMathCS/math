# Level 5: Sets and arguments

*Logic and puzzles · level 5 of 8 · rough age 12-13 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 4 you wrote statements with letters and checked them with truth tables. In this level the same ideas grow in
two directions. First, **sets**: collections of things, pictured with Venn diagrams, where *or*, *and* and *not*
become union, intersection and complement. Second, **arguments**: how "for all" and "there exists" work, which steps
of reasoning are always safe, and which tempting steps are traps.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Sets](01-sets.md) | List and describe sets, use $\in$ and $\subseteq$ correctly, explain the empty set, count subsets, and place numbers in $\mathbb{N}$, $\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{Q}$, $\mathbb{R}$ |
| [Union, intersection, complement](02-union-intersection.md) | Combine sets, shade regions of two- and three-set Venn diagrams, and see each region as a row of a truth table |
| [De Morgan's laws](03-de-morgan.md) | Prove De Morgan's laws three ways, and write the opposite of *and*, *or*, inequalities and *if* sentences correctly |
| [Quantifiers](04-quantifiers.md) | Read and write $\forall$ and $\exists$, decide when they are true, negate them, and explain why their order matters |
| [Valid arguments](05-valid-arguments.md) | Recognise modus ponens, modus tollens, chains and syllogisms, check validity with truth tables, and solve Lewis Carroll's puzzles |
| [Fallacies](06-fallacies.md) | Spot affirming the consequent and denying the antecedent, disprove a bad argument with a counterexample, and tell valid from sound |
