# Logic and puzzles

Logic is the art of working out what *must* be true from what you already know. It starts with riddles, liars and
Sudoku, and it ends with the tools every mathematician uses to be sure: statements, sets and proofs.

| Step | Page | You will be able to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Play | [Riddles, clues and Sudoku](01-play.md) | Solve puzzles by ruling things out, and spot when a clue makes something impossible |
| Explore | [Knights, knaves and true or false](02-explore.md) | Solve truth-teller and liar puzzles by trying every case, and use *not*, *and* and *or* carefully |
| Build | [Statements and truth tables](03-build.md) | Write statements with symbols, check them with truth tables, and handle *if ... then* correctly |
| Master | [Sets and "for all, there exists"](04-master.md) | Work with sets and Venn diagrams, count with overlaps, and say exactly what "every" and "some" mean |
| Beyond | [How a proof works](05-beyond.md) | Prove things directly, by contrapositive and by contradiction, and break false claims with a counterexample |
