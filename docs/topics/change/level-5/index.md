# Level 5: Slope as a rate

*Change and motion · level 5 of 8 · rough age 12-13 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 4 you found rates of change from tables. In this level the rate becomes something you can **see**: the slope
of a graph. The slope of a distance-time graph is a speed, the slope of a cost graph is a price, and the slope of a
speed-time graph is an acceleration. You learn to read the sign of a slope as part of a story, to find the average
rate along a curve with a secant line, and to work out how things fall on Earth and on Mars, ending with a surprise:
the area under a speed-time graph is the distance travelled.
