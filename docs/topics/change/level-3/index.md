# Level 3: Rates and graphs that tell stories

*Change and motion · level 3 of 8 · rough age 9-10 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 2 you measured how much things changed. In this level you measure **how fast** they change, using one
small word: *per*. You learn to read graphs as stories (where someone stopped, when the bath was emptied), to spot
steady change in a table of numbers, and to see the curve of something that changes fast at first and then slower.
The level ends with a famous trap: why the average of two speeds is usually not the average speed.
