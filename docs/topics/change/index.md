# Change and motion

Things move, grow, heat up and slow down. This topic is about measuring **how fast** something changes: first by
comparing, then with speed and slopes, and finally with the two big ideas of calculus, the derivative and the
area under a curve.

| Step | Page | You will be able to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Play | [Fast and slow](01-play.md) | Compare speeds fairly, tell speeding up from slowing down, and compare how steep slopes are |
| Explore | [Speed, distance and time](02-explore.md) | Use speed = distance ÷ time, change units, read distance-time graphs and find a true average speed |
| Build | [Slopes and rates of change](03-build.md) | Find the slope of a line, write its equation, and work out an average rate of change on any graph |
| Master | [Speed at an instant: the derivative](04-master.md) | Shrink a time interval to find the speed at one moment, and find derivatives of powers of $x$ |
| Beyond | [Areas under curves](05-beyond.md) | Turn speed into distance as an area, add up thin rectangles, and use the fundamental theorem of calculus |
