# Level 1: Growing and moving

*Change and motion · level 1 of 8 · rough age 5-6 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Everything around us changes: babies grow, ice melts, balloons go flat, the sky goes dark at night. In this level you
learn to **notice** change and to talk about it with care: what changed, what stayed the same, which came first, and
whether the change was fast or slow. These are the very first ideas of a big part of mathematics, which grown-ups
later call calculus. Every lesson has pictures, little experiments to try at home, and questions with answers.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Bigger and smaller over time](01-bigger-smaller.md) | Put pictures of something growing or shrinking in order from first to last, and explain how you knew |
| [Fast and slow](02-fast-and-slow.md) | Say who is faster in a race by looking at how far each one went in the same time |
| [Before, now and after](03-before-now-after.md) | Tell what changed and what stayed the same, like when ice melts or a balloon loses air |
| [Filling and emptying](04-filling-emptying.md) | Tell if something fills or empties the same all the way, or fast at first and then slower |
| [Changes that come back](05-changes-come-back.md) | Spot changes that repeat, like day and night, the seasons and the shape of the Moon |
| [Steep and gentle](06-steep-and-gentle.md) | Compare a steep ramp and a gentle ramp, and say which one makes a ball go faster |
