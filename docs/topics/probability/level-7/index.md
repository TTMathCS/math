# Level 7: Random variables and expected value

*Probability · level 7 of 8 · rough age 15-16 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Until now, probability has been about single events: will it happen or not? In this level chance produces **numbers**:
the total on two dice, the score on a guessed quiz, the number of rolls until a six. A random variable and its
distribution describe all those numbers at once; the expected value says what they average in the long run, and the
standard deviation says how far they stray. Then come the famous distributions (binomial, geometric, hypergeometric,
uniform and a first look at Poisson), each with a proof of where its formula comes from and a guide to choosing the
right one.
