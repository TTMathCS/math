# Level 6: Two variables

*Statistics and data · level 6 of 8 · rough age 13-14 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Until now every data set has been one list of numbers. In this level each person or thing gives you **two** numbers,
such as hours studied and test score, and the question becomes: do they move together? You draw scatter plots,
describe correlation in words, fit a straight line by eye and use it to predict, and learn when a prediction is safe
and when it is nonsense. You also meet the most important warning in statistics, that correlation is not causation.
The level ends with the standard deviation, the measure of spread that the rest of statistics is built on.
