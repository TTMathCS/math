# Level 5: Spread and samples

*Statistics and data · level 5 of 8 · rough age 12-13 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 4 you chose a fair average and drew data honestly. In this level you look past the middle: you cut data into
quarters, draw the famous box plot, and use a simple rule to decide which values are truly unusual. Then the level turns
to the biggest question in statistics: how can a **sample** of a few hundred people tell us about millions, and how
does a sample go wrong? You learn the main ways to choose a sample, and the kinds of bias that have fooled even
famous polls.

| [Bias](06-bias.md) | Name sampling, non-response, response and measurement bias, and explain how the 1936 Literary Digest poll went wrong |
