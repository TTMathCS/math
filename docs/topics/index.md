# Math by topic

Each topic is a ladder. It starts with something you can play with and climbs, one step at a time, to the math taught
at the end of high school. Start at the bottom, or jump in at any step that looks right.

| Step | What it feels like |
| --- | --- |
| **Play** | Puzzles and things to notice. No formulas needed. |
| **Explore** | Putting what you noticed into words and numbers. |
| **Build** | The first real tools: rules, formulas, and why they work. |
| **Master** | Using the tools on harder problems. |
| **Beyond** | Where the topic leads: proofs and the math after high school. |

For parents and teachers: Play suits most children from about 6, Explore from about 9, Build from about 12, Master
from about 14, and Beyond from about 16. These are rough guides; curious readers ignore them.
