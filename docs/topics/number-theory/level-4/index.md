# Level 4: GCD, LCM and Euclid

*Number theory · level 4 of 8 · rough age 11-12 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 3 you broke numbers into primes. In this level you compare **two** numbers: the biggest number that divides
both (the greatest common divisor) and the smallest number both divide (the least common multiple). You meet a method
from Euclid's *Elements*, written around 300 BC, that finds the greatest common divisor without factorizing anything
and still runs inside computers today, and his proof that the primes never run out. The level ends with perfect
numbers, where a question from ancient Greece is still unanswered.

| [Least common multiple](02-least-common-multiple.md) | Find the LCM by listing multiples and from prime factorizations, and use it for repeating cycles and adding fractions |
