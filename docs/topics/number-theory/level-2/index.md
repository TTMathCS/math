# Level 2: Factors and multiples

*Number theory · level 2 of 8 · rough age 7-8 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 1 you made pairs, shared things out and built rectangles from counters. In this level those ideas get
their real names. Rectangles become **factors**, skip counting becomes **multiples**, and the line-only numbers
become **primes**. You will find out why odd plus odd is always even, find every prime up to 100 with a 2,000-year-old
method, and solve a famous puzzle about 100 lockers.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Odd and even sums](01-odd-even-sums.md) | Say whether a sum is odd or even without adding, and explain why with pairs of blocks |
| [Multiples](02-multiples.md) | List multiples, find their patterns on a hundred chart, and find where two skip counts land together |
| [Factors with arrays](03-factors-arrays.md) | Find every factor of a number with rectangles, and list factors in pairs without missing any |
| [Primes and composites](04-primes-composites.md) | Tell primes from composites, explain why 1 is not prime, and know the primes up to 50 |
| [The sieve of Eratosthenes](05-sieve-eratosthenes.md) | Find all the primes up to 100 on a hundred chart, and explain why you can stop after crossing out the 7s |
| [Square numbers and the locker puzzle](06-square-numbers-lockers.md) | Explain why square numbers have an odd number of factors, and solve the 100 lockers puzzle |
