# Level 1: Odd, even and sharing

*Number theory · level 1 of 8 · rough age 5-6 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Number theory is the part of math about whole numbers: 1, 2, 3, 4, and so on. It starts with a question you can
answer with socks and sweets: does everything have a partner, or is one left over? In this level you learn odd and
even, fair sharing, skip counting, the numbers that make rectangles, and what a leftover is. Every lesson has
pictures, things to try with real objects, and answers to check.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Pairs: everything has a partner](01-pairs-partners.md) | Line things up two by two and say if a number is even (all partners) or odd (one left over) |
| [Odd and even on the number line](02-odd-even-line.md) | See that odd and even take turns, and tell any number's kind from its last digit |
| [Fair sharing between two friends](03-fair-sharing-two.md) | Share counters fairly between two people, and know before you start whether one will be left |
| [Skip counting by 2s, 5s and 10s](04-skip-counting.md) | Skip count on a number line, with hands and with nickels, and say which numbers you land on |
| [Rectangle numbers](05-rectangle-numbers.md) | Build rectangles from counters, and find which numbers up to 12 make a rectangle and which only make a line |
| [Leftovers](06-leftovers.md) | Share among 3, 4 or 5 friends, find the leftover, and explain why it is always smaller than the number of friends |
