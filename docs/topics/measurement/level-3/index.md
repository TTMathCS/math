# Level 3: Metric system, time and area

*Measurement · level 3 of 8 · rough age 9-10 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 2 you measured with centimetres, metres, kilograms and litres, and read clocks. In this level the units
become a family: milli-, centi- and kilo- link them by 10, 100 and 1000, so changing units is as easy as moving a
decimal point, and you learn **why**. You work out how long things take on any clock, including the 24-hour one,
start measuring the space inside shapes (area), and read thermometers below zero, all the way down to the cold of
Mars.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [The metric family](01-metric-family.md) | Use mm, cm, m and km, change between them by multiplying or dividing by 10, 100 or 1000, and explain why the decimal point moves |
| [Mass and capacity](02-mass-capacity.md) | Use grams, kilograms, millilitres and litres, choose a sensible unit, read a scale, and convert |
| [Elapsed time and the 24-hour clock](03-elapsed-time.md) | Find how long something takes with a time line, read and write 24-hour times, and change between hours, minutes and seconds |
| [Area by counting squares](04-area-counting-squares.md) | Measure area in square centimetres and square metres, count half squares, and find shapes with the same area but different perimeters |
| [Area of rectangles](05-area-rectangles.md) | Find a rectangle's area by multiplying, explain why length × width works, and find a missing side |
| [Temperature in degrees Celsius](06-temperature-celsius.md) | Read a thermometer above and below zero, find temperature differences, and compare places, seasons and planets |
