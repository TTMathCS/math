# Level 2: Units for everyone

*Measurement · level 2 of 8 · rough age 7-8 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 1 you measured with hands, feet and blocks, and found that everyone's hand gives a different number. In
this level everyone uses the **same** units: centimetres and metres for length, kilograms for mass, litres for
capacity, hours and minutes on the clock, and days, weeks, months and years on the calendar. You learn to use a ruler
properly, to estimate before you measure, to read scales and clocks, and to walk all the way round a shape to find its
perimeter.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Why we need the same units](01-same-units.md) | Explain why body units cause trouble, use centimetres and metres, and measure with a ruler starting at 0 (or from any mark) |
| [Measuring and estimating length](02-estimating-length.md) | Use benchmarks like a finger (about 1 cm) and a big step (about 1 m) to estimate, then measure and judge how close you were |
| [Kilograms and litres](03-kilograms-litres.md) | Picture a kilogram and a litre, read simple scales and jugs, and know that a litre of water weighs about a kilogram |
| [Telling time](04-telling-time.md) | Read analogue and digital clocks to five minutes, and use o'clock, half past, quarter past and quarter to |
| [The calendar](05-the-calendar.md) | Use days, weeks, months and years, find how many days a month has with the knuckle trick, and explain leap years |
| [Perimeter](06-perimeter.md) | Find the distance around a shape by adding its sides or counting on a grid, find a missing side, and make different shapes with the same perimeter |
