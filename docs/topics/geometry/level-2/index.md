# Level 2: Shapes all around

*Geometry · level 2 of 8 · rough age 7-8 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

Shapes are everywhere: in stop signs, honeycombs, dice, cereal boxes and bathroom floors. In this level you name
polygons with up to 10 sides and learn what makes one regular, find lines of symmetry by folding, count the faces,
edges and corners of solids, unfold a cube in all 11 possible ways, discover which shapes tile a floor (and prove
why regular pentagons never do), and learn the three moves of geometry: slides, flips and turns.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Polygons and regular shapes](01-regular-polygons.md) | Name polygons from 3 to 10 sides, test whether one is regular, and count its diagonals |
| [Lines of symmetry](02-lines-of-symmetry.md) | Find every line of symmetry of a shape by folding or with a mirror, and finish a mirror picture |
| [Faces, edges and corners](03-faces-edges-corners.md) | Count the faces, edges and vertices of solids with a plan, and check them with Euler's rule |
| [Nets of a cube](04-nets-of-a-cube.md) | Recognise all 11 nets of a cube, find opposite faces, and explain why some shapes cannot fold |
| [Tiling the floor](05-tiling-the-floor.md) | Explain which shapes tile a floor with no gaps, and why regular pentagons and octagons cannot |
| [Slides, flips and turns](06-slides-flips-turns.md) | Describe a slide, a flip or a turn exactly, and tell a flip from a half turn |
