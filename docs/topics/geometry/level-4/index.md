# Level 4: Angle facts

*Geometry · level 4 of 8 · rough age 11-12 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 3 you measured angles. In this level you work them out exactly, without a protractor, and you learn **why**
each fact is true. A handful of facts (straight lines, crossing lines, parallel lines) lock together to prove that a
triangle's angles add to 180°, to find the angles of any polygon, and to show why only three regular shapes can tile
a floor. The level ends with symmetry by turning.

| Lesson | You will be able to |
| --- | --- |
| [Angles on a straight line and around a point](01-straight-line-point.md) | Find missing angles using 180° and 360°, and solve angle equations with letters |
| [Vertically opposite angles](02-vertically-opposite.md) | Prove that opposite angles at a crossing are equal, and use it to find all four angles from one |
| [Angles in a triangle add to 180°](03-triangle-angle-sum.md) | Show the 180° fact three ways, and use it and the exterior angle fact to find missing angles |
| [Angles with parallel lines](04-parallel-line-angles.md) | Use corresponding, alternate and co-interior angles, and follow Euclid's proof of the 180° fact |
| [Angles inside and outside any polygon](05-polygon-angles.md) | Find the angle sum of any polygon, the angles of any regular polygon, and explain why only three regular polygons tile |
| [Rotational symmetry](06-rotational-symmetry.md) | Find the order and angle of rotational symmetry of a shape, and tell it apart from line symmetry |
