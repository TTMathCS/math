# Geometry

Geometry is the mathematics of shape and space: how shapes fit together, how big their angles are, how long their
sides must be. It starts with folding paper and fitting pieces together, and climbs to the tools that measure the
height of a mountain you cannot climb and steer a rover across another planet.

| Step | Page | You will be able to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Play | [Shapes, tangrams and symmetry](01-play.md) | Name shapes, build pictures from tangram pieces, and find lines of symmetry by folding |
| Explore | [Angles and polygons](02-explore.md) | Measure and name angles, use the angle facts for lines and triangles, and find the angles of any polygon |
| Build | [Pythagoras and circles](03-build.md) | Find any side of a right triangle, the distance between two points, and the circumference and area of a circle |
| Master | [Similar triangles and trigonometry](04-master.md) | Use scale factors, sine, cosine and tangent, and the sine and cosine laws to find lengths and angles you cannot measure |
| Beyond | [Geometric proof and vectors](05-beyond.md) | Write a proof, use angles in radians on the unit circle, and add, scale and multiply vectors |
