# Geometry

Geometry is the mathematics of shape and space: how shapes fit together, how big their angles are, how long their
sides must be. It starts with folding paper and fitting pieces together, and climbs to the tools that measure the
height of a mountain you cannot climb and steer a rover across another planet.

8 levels, 48 lessons. The ages are only a rough guide for parents and teachers; anyone can start at any level.
