# Patterns and sequences

A pattern is something that repeats or grows by a rule. Find the rule and you can tell what comes next, what comes
a hundred steps later, and sometimes what happens if it goes on forever.

| Step | Page | You will be able to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Play | [What comes next?](01-play.md) | Spot repeating and growing patterns and continue them |
| Explore | [Number sequences and their rules](02-explore.md) | Describe a sequence with a rule and a table; meet square, triangle and Fibonacci numbers |
| Build | [Adding the same amount: arithmetic sequences](03-build.md) | Find any term with a formula and add up a long list fast |
| Master | [Multiplying by the same amount: geometric sequences](04-master.md) | Work with doubling and halving, growth and decay, and sums that go on forever |
| Beyond | [Rules that call themselves, and proof by induction](05-beyond.md) | Use recursion, meet the golden ratio and prove a pattern is true for every number |
