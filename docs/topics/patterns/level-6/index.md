# Level 6: Geometric sequences

*Patterns and sequences · level 6 of 8 · rough age 13-14 (a guide for parents; anyone can start anywhere)*

In level 5 you **added** the same amount each time. In this level you **multiply** by the same amount each time, and
the patterns behave completely differently: they explode (rice on a chessboard, paper folded to the Moon), shrink
towards zero, or flip sign back and forth. You learn the formula for any term, how to tell arithmetic from geometric
from neither, how to add up a geometric sequence with one clever subtraction, how to work with rules that build each
term from the ones before, and how to choose the right kind of sequence for a real situation.

| [Recursive sequences](05-recursive-sequences.md) | Compute terms of rules like $t_n = 3t_{n-1} - 1$ in a table or spreadsheet, find where they settle, and turn some into position rules |
