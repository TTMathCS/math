# Math, shelf by shelf

Each shelf holds a set of courses. A course goes chapter by chapter: what each idea means, why the theorems are
true, worked examples, the usual mistakes, and practice questions with their answers.

The math also reaches Mars: the path from Earth to Mars in the picture above is half an ellipse round the Sun, and
its 259 days come from Kepler's third law, which Newton explained with calculus.
