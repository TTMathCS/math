# Combinatorics

*A book in 20 chapters about counting, arranging and connecting: from "how many ways?" to graphs, Ramsey theory,
the probabilistic method and the codes that carried pictures home from Mars. Each chapter starts from a question,
proves what it claims, works examples step by step, and ends with questions whose answers are folded underneath.*

## How to read it

- **In order.** Each chapter needs only the ones before it; the **Next** link at the end of every chapter walks you
  through.
- **Levels inside a chapter.** The box at the top of each chapter says which sections are beginner, intermediate,
  advanced or expert. On a first read, skip the harder sections and come back later.
- **Slower help.** The first chapters point to the matching lessons on the [Topics](../../topics/counting/index.md)
  shelf, which teach the same ideas more gently, for younger readers.
- **Check by listing.** For small cases, write every arrangement out, or let a few lines of Python do it; most
  chapters show how.

## Three ways in

- **Starting out:** read chapters 1 to 5. They are the counting every later chapter uses.
- **For computing and puzzles:** chapters 6 to 8, then the graphs of chapters 10 to 14.
- **You know some algebra and probability:** read chapters 15 to 20. The [Algebra](../algebra/index.md) book's
  group chapters and the [Number theory](../number-theory/index.md) book's partitions chapter are the companions.
